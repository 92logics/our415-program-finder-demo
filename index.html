        About this demonstration

        This is a demonstration built by 92 Logics for the San Francisco DCYF 2027 Our415 Request for Proposals (Service Area 1: Web Design & Development). It is not a City and County of San Francisco, DCYF or Our415.org website, and it is not endorsed by them.

        Data

        Listings are read from the public SF Open Data dataset Our415 Events and Activities (8i3s-ih2a), licensed under the Open Data Commons PDDL. The page loads a snapshot taken on 2 Nov 2026 and then refreshes from the live Socrata API in your browser. Listings may be out of date; always confirm with the provider.

        "Category" here is a demo-derived topic computed with simple keyword rules over the event name and description. It is not the Our415 taxonomy. Age groups are normalised from the dataset's age eligibility tags.

        Search

        The plain-language search runs entirely in your browser. A rule-based intent parser picks out an age, cost, topic, neighborhood, day and time of day from what you type (including some Spanish, Chinese and Filipino words), shows what it understood as removable chips, and widens the search if nothing matches. In production this layer could call a semantic or AI search service, with DCYF approving how AI is used.

        Languages

        Interface text is available in English, Spanish, Chinese, Filipino and Vietnamese. These are sample translations for demonstration, not reviewed by City translators. Listing content is shown in its source language.

        Saved programs

        Saving and preferences use this browser's local storage as a stand-in for user accounts. Nothing is sent to any server. No analytics or tracking are used.

        Accessibility

        Built to WCAG 2.1 AA practices: skip link, landmarks, labelled controls, visible focus, live regions for result counts, keyboard-operable everything, sufficient colour contrast, a larger-text option, reduced-motion support, and a list equivalent for every map marker. Automated axe-core checks run in our test suite. Map tiles © OpenStreetMap contributors.
